How the forecast is built

Data

Walmart Store Sales (Kaggle, yasserh/walmart-dataset) — 6,435 weekly records across 45 stores, Feb 2010 to Dec 2012. Includes weekly sales, holiday flag, temperature, fuel price, CPI, and unemployment.

Feature engineering

Per store: sales lags at 1/4/8/12 weeks, rolling mean & std over 4/8/12 weeks (computed on a 1-week-shifted window so a week's own sales never leak into its own average), plus month, week-of-year, and quarter.

Split

Time-based, never random — the model is always trained on the past and evaluated on the future. 80% train / 20% test by date.

Known limitations

  • Forecasts beyond the historical data range are recursive — each predicted week feeds the next, so uncertainty compounds the further out you forecast. Treat horizons beyond ~8 weeks as directional, not precise.
  • Exogenous inputs (temperature, fuel price, CPI, unemployment) are carried forward from the last known value unless you override them on the Forecast page — the model has no way to know these in advance.
  • The model was trained on 2010–2012 data; store openings/closures, macro shocks, or structural changes in shopping behavior since then are not reflected.
  • Store-level accuracy varies — check the Store Log page for a given store's actual-vs-predicted fit before trusting its forecast closely.
